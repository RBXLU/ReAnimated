--- Compatibility notes ---

Smooth Scrolling / SmoothScroll / Smooth Scrolling Refurbished
    Items disappeared while scrolling the creative inventory on Minecraft 1.21.1-1.21.3.
    Fixed in 1.6: ReAnimated now shifts the clipping rectangle by its own animation only,
    instead of by whatever is currently in the drawing matrix.

Rechiseled (+ SuperMartijn642's Core Lib)
    The block list rendered empty. Same cause, fixed in 1.4.1 / 1.4.3.

Mods that draw a GUI in their own coordinate system are the risky ones in general: they set
clipping in local coordinates and expect the vanilla behaviour of the version they target.
If something in a modded GUI disappears while ReAnimated is installed, report the Minecraft
version and the mod - and as a workaround, General -> "Animate everything / Vanilla only"
limits the mod to vanilla screens, and "Animate lists" turns off list animation separately.
